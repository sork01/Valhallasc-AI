## The shape

At k = 1.0 the atlas outline is grid 296 with 41,194 land tiles (the old square was 160, 25,600): a wedge. Borders: the Yearwheel on the curved north-west side (131 tiles, **the wall**), the sea along the long north-east diagonal (295, coast), the Veyrroot Canopy's jungle along the south (286) and Sunward Vale along the west (178). None of the three neighbours is on the pilot, so **there is no seam**: `world/zone-shapes.txt` has `designOnly` + `rebuild` (the old square is not moved; `lapis_design.restore_and_shift` brings the old people, places and portals back from `preshape-25.txt`) and `seamWith {25: []}`. **The day Sunward Vale, the Veyrroot Canopy or the Yearwheel is shaped, its design must list 25 in its own `seamWith` and this design must learn the seam** (reserve the border first, see the other pilot docs).

## The Wheel Wall (the Yearwheel has no crossing)

- `lapis_design.Lapis.build_wall` lays 59 `rampart` pieces and 6 `tower`s along the whole Yearwheel border run, 1.7 tiles inside it, one piece every 2 tiles of arc length, a tower at both ends and about every 28 tiles. Pieces are axis-aligned boxes as in `zone_shapes.build_walls` (2.8x1.8 where the border runs more along x, 1.8x2.8 where more along y, 2.3x2.3 between) and overlap, so nothing slips between them. Rampart colour sandstone `#d7c8a0`, tower roof lapis blue `#2f6f9a`. Real collision, real isometric art (stone courses, merlons, a pennant on every tower).
- **No gate, no arch, no portal.** `restore_and_shift` drops `atlas_25_28`; `close_yearwheel` (runs after `compact_atlas` on every full sync-world, idempotent) removes the Yearwheel's `atlas_28_25`, both exit routes, the Yearwheel's trail that led to it, the `{25, 28}` entry of `world['atlas']['links']` and the Spark links between Azure Wells and the Yearwheel's Solhaven master. The painted border is still the border: the ground beyond the wall is drawn as the neighbour's edge.
- Nothing stands within 3.5 tiles of the wall, no spawn within 10, the ground within 9 tiles is red rock (`wallside` region, "The Wheel Wall Verge"). Wallwatch Post (the north outpost) is the post that "keeps the wall"; its warden Hesper explains it.
- The Yearwheel stays reachable from Sunward Vale, Frostwake and the Cairnspine. `compact_atlas_tests` now expect 47 painted borders (was 48) and 94 directed paths (was 96).

## New elite: the Cobalt Basilisk (`cobaltbasilisk`)

Level 83, no quest, material `basilisk_scale` ("Cobalt Basilisk Scale"). A huge desert lizard: lapis-blue scales with brass studs, a sand-gold belly, a sail of brass spines on a cobalt membrane down its back, two swept brass horns on a wedge skull, one burning orange slit eye under a heavy brow and a jaw of bone fangs. Winds up by dropping its chest and rearing the head back and up with the jaws wide and the sail flared; the strike snaps the neck forward and down; it dies when its legs splay and the body sinks onto the sand, the colour draining to grey from the tail forward. `scripts/lapis_elite_redraw.py` (native PixelFlow source `1dcfb029f810a3da`, frame 192 x 84, eight distinct attack and eight distinct death frames), manifest `client/assets/lapiselites.txt` (the six old enemies and the five NPCs keep `lapis.txt`). Stats are a first guess: 3,300,000 HP, 25,500 damage, speed 2.3, awareness 15, enrage `[180, 0.2, 3.0]`.
