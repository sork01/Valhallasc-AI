# Moonwell visual guide

The Moonwell is a floating nocturnal garden, open to a sky reflected in deep water. Its route is a chain of irregular lily islands and narrow glass causeways. The shape language is curved, botanical and airy; avoid stone halls, crypt walls and orange torchlight.

Palette: ink `#15263b`, deep water `#153d58`, silver leaf `#a8d5cf`, mint light `#9af2d7`, lilac shadow `#8276b5`, moon gold `#f8e8ad`, coral accent `#f0a6be`. Moon gold marks safe paths and rewards; coral marks danger. Light falls from the upper left and casts cool violet shadows. The water and sky stay quieter than creatures and interactable objects.

Each creature and prop is a separate asset. The Moonwell monster manifest is Dew Moth, Lumen Eel, Rootbell, Tideglass Heron, Hourpetal Stag, Moonskein Weaver, and Moonwell Echo. Each has its own transparent atlas with six idle, eight walk, eight attack, four hurt and eight death frames. The existing Echo atlas stays intact. World props are individual code-rendered moon lilies, mirror pools and reeds, placed from the seeded map generator. The sky, water and shore are drawn in distinct layers from the same camera position.
