# Player stun and Arcane Ward effects

`FX-STUN-WARD` uses authoritative player snapshots for both the local hero and remote players. It changes presentation only.

- `stun` and `root` are seconds remaining. `wound` is the healing reduction fraction; `woundLeft` is its remaining duration. Stun, root and wound appear as labelled statuses with seconds in the existing buff HUD. They clear when the server clears them, including at duel end.
- Stun draws three gold stars circling a ring above the character for its entire duration, plus a brief impact flare on application. Reduced motion holds the ring and stars still.
- Arcane Ward uses five floating, faceted ice clusters and a soft blue frost aura, following the owner's FileDrop reference `1159378-ice-barrier.jpg`. It draws while its `arcaneward` shield buff **or** its positive `absorb` pool remains. Its soak bar shows the remaining pool relative to `maxHp * WORLD_SKILLS.arcaneward.absorb`; the numeric label shows the amount left. The readout draws over nearby actors so a monster in front cannot hide it. The ice becomes fainter as the pool falls.
- A decrease in the pool, or health loss while warded, flashes the ice white. Exhaustion of the pool and expiry of the remaining ward each produce a short burst of ice fragments. The ice barrier remains visible if its shield buff is still active after the pool breaks. `absorbLeft` distinguishes an expired pool from a consumed one.
- Reduced motion holds the ice clusters still and uses static impact/burst shapes. The ice facets and frost gradients are drawn into cached canvases once; the existing render loop only blits them, with early exits for unaffected actors; there are no extra animation loops, filters or compositor hints.

The two additive snapshot fields (`woundLeft`, `absorbLeft`) require a server release. The client remains compatible with older snapshots: stun/root and ward visuals still work, but wound's countdown needs `woundLeft`.

## Rear-hit daze

Enemy attacks that land from the rear half of the player's facing direction have a 40% chance to apply Dazed for four seconds. Daze halves normal walking speed, including walking to a target. The enemy hit dismounts the victim before applying daze, through the existing authoritative combat path. It does not stop attacks, skills or dashes. A new successful application refreshes the duration to four seconds; the slow never stacks. Dodges, immunity, completely absorbed hits, environmental damage and PvP attacks do not apply daze. Death clears it.

The server uses a facing dot product against the incoming attack direction. Melee and slam attacks use the enemy's position; projectiles use their incoming flight direction so crossing the victim within one tick cannot reverse front and rear. `Player.daze` is transient, ticks down on the server and is sent as `daze` in player snapshots. The client shares the stun stars and landing flare for local and remote players, with reduced motion supported, and shows a separate Dazed countdown and 50% movement tooltip. Swedish: Omtöcknad.

Focused checks: `bash scripts/cargo.sh test daze_ -- --nocapture` and `VALHALLA_BINARY=<candidate-release> bash scripts/test-touch-bounded.sh tests/daze-ui.cjs`. These cover the 40% boundary, front/side/rear hits, projectile crossings, refresh and expiry, exact half-speed walking, actions during daze, rejected hits, death cleanup, authoritative HUD and overhead pixels for the victim and observer. No encounter timing or win-rate sweep is required.
