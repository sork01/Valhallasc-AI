# Duel matrix: every class against every class

Generated by `bash scripts/duel-parallel.sh` (`PVP-DUEL-MATRIX`) from real duels in isolated in-memory worlds; this report is input to later balancing and changes no game stat.

## Method

- Code under test: the real duel (`server/src/world/pvp.rs`): challenge and accept, the 25-tile ring, a blow that would take a duelist to 0 leaves 1 health and ends the duel, no loot, no XP. Harness: `server/src/world/duel_tests.rs`.
- Characters: both sides at the same level, in the level's normal green progression gear (no refinement, no set bonus, no potions or food), the even default attribute allocation, every skill and rank earned by the level. Same gear tier as the DPS report.
- **Skills hurt players in duels** (the damaging skills of `use_skill` and their bolts and dashes now resolve against the duel partner, the opposing arena team and flagged players through `pvp_hit` in `pvp.rs`; before 2026-10-20 only the basic attack could). A skill blow uses the same rules as the basic blow: defence is subtracted, damage is scaled by 0.25, shield and dodge apply, and a duelist stops at 1 health. A warrior gains rage from the damage it deals, as against monsters.
- **PvP rules in force** (all in `pvp.rs` and `use_skill`, PvP only; monsters are unaffected): every blow that lands on a player is scaled by `PVP_DAMAGE_SCALE` 0.5 on top of the monster scale of 0.25. Skills with a status: a **3 s stun** (no moving, attacking or casting) from Warrior Earthshatter (15 s cooldown), Assassin Knife Ring (14 s) and Hunter Rain of Arrows (12 s); a **3 s root** (no moving or dashing) from Mage Arcane Storm (15 s); a **heal cut of 50% for 10 s** (skill heals, potions, food, set heals) from Warrior Titan's Wrath (40 s), Assassin Thousand Cuts (28 s), Mage Starfall (36 s) and Hunter Arrow Storm (30 s). A status needs a blow that lands (a dodge or miss applies nothing), does not stack (the longer wins) and ends with the duel. **Healing is scaled by the same 0.5** for a player in a PvP fight (a blow dealt or taken in the last 10 s; it clears when the duel ends), or halving the damage would double what healing is worth. **A hunter's blows on players deal 55% more** (owner, 2026-10-20: +25%, then +10%, then +20%). **A priest's own healing is scaled once more by 0.5** in a PvP fight (a quarter in all). **Lifesteal (Titan's Wrath, Life Drain, Searing Light) heals nothing** while the caster is in a PvP fight. **Mage Arcane Ward (28 s)** also soaks up the next 20% of the mage's maximum health in damage from players for 10 s.
- Rotation: **the mercenaries' own code** (`World::ai_combat_at`, the body of `ai_combat`): every skill when ready, affordable and in reach, buffs when none is active and the foe is within 10 tiles, a heal below 55% health, no dashes. The harness only walks to 90% of the class's reach (a mercenary's stand-off) and swings the basic attack (`PvpCommand::Attack`, reach 1.8 for the melee classes, 8 for mage and hunter) whenever its cooldown allows. This is not the best rotation of `docs/best-rotations.md`. The caveats of the DPS report apply: the mercenaries' code starves assassin and hunter of energy and never fires dashes (`DPS-ROTATION`), so their results are a floor.
- Start: 10 tiles apart on a flat clear line in Greenmeadow, the monsters of that zone removed. The pair is run in both orders (class A left with session 1, then right with session 2; session 1 acts first within a tick, so each class has that one-tick edge half the time). A mirror match (same class) is run on one order with twice the seeds; its figure is the share won by session 1.
- Seeds: 8 duels per pair and level (crits, hit rolls, dodges and damage spread are random; seeds 1..n). A duel that has no winner after 300 s of game time is a draw and counts half a win.
- Numbers: win rate = (wins + half of draws) / duels. Time = seconds from accept to the last blow, over decided duels: median and the shortest to longest. Damage = health points taken off the other side by each source (the 1-health floor of a duel respected), from the server's own accounting. Casts = buff and heal casts, from the server's own count.
- Caveats: the figures describe two scripted players, not skilled play: no kiting, no dashes or escapes, no potions, no target-ground meteor aiming (a meteor lands on the nearest foe in range), no skill choice by situation. A human warrior closing on a mage, or a hunter running away, would shift the result. Gear is the level's green set; purple/orange gear, set bonuses and rebirth perks are not modelled. The mercenaries' code stands still at 90% of reach, so a ranged class never kites a melee one.
