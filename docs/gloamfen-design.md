# Gloamfen as an authored map (zone shapes pilot, 2026-11-06)

Owner request: "redesign gloamveil. keep shape and borders etc. but redesign the rest of the map and the quests. to not have any filler land and to use the entire map". "Gloamveil" was read as Gloamfen (zone 3), the next pilot zone after Greenmeadow, the Crags and Rimeveil. Pilot only (branch `feat/zone-shapes-gloam`, off `feat/zone-shapes`). The same choices as the other three: three outposts, three elites (two with a quest, one without), every old quest id kept and rewritten.

## What stays

The outline, the seams to Rimeveil (north) and Greenmeadow (south-east), Skaldholm's wall and vault (east), Lanternmere and its eight people, the spawn point, the Ember Grave Cross and its sleeper, the Empty Seat circle, the Halloween ritual (lantern, pad, Hollow Rider's sleeper), the Gloomroot Colossus, and every quest id (18 `fen_*`, `es_04`, `es_go_04`).

## How it is built

- `scripts/gloamfen_design.py` runs in `zone_shapes.py apply` right after Rimeveil's design (`if 3 in cfg['zones']`), after `build_walls`. `class Gloamfen(Designer)` reuses `greenmeadow_design.py`'s helpers; data in `scripts/gloamfen_content.py`. `python3 scripts/gloamfen_design.py plan OUT.png` draws it read-only. Gloamfen is `world['zones'][2]` (the file list starts at zone 1).
- The plan traces the painted atlas (`tile = painted px * 2048/1758 - shape.T`, T = [123,577]): the brown Old Quarter plateau and Lanternmere on the northern rise, the black stream that runs west under the town and bends into the Mere, the Mere (a C of water round a green isle), the winding brown road down the west bank, the lanterns, the dark thickets of the south-east.
- **Where water may stand** is decided by what the neighbours draw. Seam ghosts and the vault view draw no `water`, so none stands within 17 tiles of a seam, and none east of **x = 222**: Skaldholm's camera is 1600 x 900 px at 88 x 44 px a tile, so it sees about 19 tiles into Gloamfen from the wall, plus the cull margin. (`shape.vaultRegions[0].land` is 90 tiles deep; Rimeveil's rule of keeping water out of all of it would have banned the river.) Asserted in `design()` (`seam_gap` 17.0, `vault_gap` 2.0) and in the Rust test `a_fen_road_reaches_every_gate_and_the_water_is_solid_with_fords`. East of the last water the stream's old bed continues as the dry **Culvert**: a dark ground class (`bed`) with stepping stones, running to the wall.
- Waters: the Mere (thick line, solid circles of r 2.3), a side channel that makes **Hydra Isle** a real island (one causeway ford at (163,170)), the stream (two fords: the Plank Ford and the Reed Ford), 60 bog pools and three tarns. A ford is the gap where a road runs within `FORD + r` of a circle; the roads stay 6+ tiles from the channel except at the causeway.
- Twelve regions, every land cell in one (nearest seed, warped by noise; Hydra Isle is the polygon the channel and the Mere enclose): The Lanternmere Commons, The Old Quarter, The Singing Bank, The Pilgrim Plank, The Mere Mouth, The Shorewalk, Hydra Isle, The Southern Dikes, The Seaward Flats, The Drowned Garrison, The Reedmarch, The Gloomroot Mire.
- Ground classes (`groundSpans`, none within 16-30 tiles of a seam): peat, blackwater mud (the bank beside every water), moss, reed bed, brown clay, shingle, bog, the culvert's bed. `fenGround`, `FEN_MINI` in `field.js`.
- Scenery stays inside the fen sprite set (willow, reeds, fen rock, column, thicket) plus `lamp`, so seam ghosts draw it; `'fen'` joined the theme list that ghosts art-drawn kinds (lamps, houses). 56 lamps at the painted lantern spots (nudged off roads and water). The Old Quarter is five rings of columns and stone, the Garrison a rectangle of tumbled stone with two gates, the dikes a line of stones with two breaks, the Seaward Flats a ferry wreck.
- Self-checks in `design()`: spawns, new people, places and every gate reachable from the spawn on foot with water solid, roads on land with nothing standing on them (the painted exit marking counts as a road), Per's escort route clear leg by leg, under 1% of open cells cut off, water clear of seams and the vault view. Two `sync-world` runs are byte-identical and no other zone changes.
- Difficulty comes only from where a kind stands: toads (15) on the Commons, the Singing Bank and the Reedmarch; crocodiles (17) along every water; knights (18) in the Garrison and the Mire; hydras (20) on the Isle and in the Mire. 234 spawns (was 61).

## Outposts, quests, elites

- **Reedhallow** (west bank, (70,100)), **Dikewatch** (south shore, (152,250)), **Garrison's Rest** (east of the Mere, (214,156)): three givers each (the third is the trader), a Travel Master linked to `travel_lanternmere`, a mailbox.
- 36 quests in the zone: 20 old ids (18 `fen_*` rewritten, some moved to outpost givers, plus `es_04`, `es_go_04`), 16 new. Level 15-20, `rewardXp = levels.txt[level-1] / 10`. Kinds: bring (toad glands, croc hides, drowned gauntlets, hydra fangs), kill, visit, talk, hold (The Failing Wicks, The Dike Breach), escort (Per Fenmark from the Rest down the east shore and over the causeway to Hydra Isle), group elite (Bellowmaw). New ids and people start `fen_` and stay within 19 characters (the accept string is capped at 32). Swedish by hand in `sv-translations.txt`, names and places in `sv-keep.txt`. Rewards in `quest_gear_rewards.py`.
- **Elites:** the Gloomroot Colossus (old, quest `fen_gloomroot`, five players) plus `bellowmaw` (quest `fen_rh_bellow`, the wet ground north-west) and `rustmarshal` (no quest, the Garrison's yard). Level 20, hard-coded in `world.rs`/`items.rs` like the Crags' and Rimeveil's elites (8200/9400 HP, first guess: the owner tests fights), respawn 180 s, enrage `[180, 0.2, 3.0]`, materials `bellowmaw_gullet`, `rustmarshal_plate`. Art `scripts/fen_elite_redraw.py` (montage, preview, extents, build, refresh, export, verify), sources `scripts/fen_elite_sources.txt`, manifest `client/assets/fenelites.txt`: a bull toad with glowing glands, a swelling throat sac and a lashing tongue; a drowned captain in rusted plate with a cage helm, cyan eyes and a barnacled anchor on a chain. Eight distinct attack and death frames each.

## Checks

`REAL_SPRITES=1 bash scripts/test-touch-bounded.sh tests/gloamfen-design-ui.cjs` (regions, outposts, elites, water; pictures in `test-results/gloamfen-design/`); `SEAMS=1` compares the same world point from both frames of each seam; `ZONES=3 tests/vault-seamless-ui.cjs` for the vault. Rust: `cargo test the_fen` plus `every_fen_quest`, `a_fen_road`, `the_failing_wicks`, `the_dike_breach`, `per_walks`.
