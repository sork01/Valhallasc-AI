# Seasonal accessories and Festival Spirit — private Beta 1.38.0

All eight seasonal follow-up TODOs are implemented in the private checkout
`/media/raid/valhallasc-data/solstice-work`, branch `feat/summer-solstice`.
This extends the earlier local/uncommitted Solstice candidate; it also integrates
New Year e6a4635 and Valentine 948a155 alongside the Easter 94a9fc6 prerequisite.
Source is local/uncommitted, not pushed, merged to main or deployed. No live
client/server/DB changes, new zones, copy-ID shift or DB migration. Mailboxes
remains a separate candidate. Rules/defaults: seasonal-events.md.

## Result

Every registered season has a dance centerpiece in all four main cities:
Halloween pumpkin, Christmas tree, New Year rocket, Valentine heart, Easter egg
and midsummer pole. Interact, approach the clear radius-1.8 ring, and automatically
dance two full ten-second circuits. The server moves the character through legal
collision-checked steps and counts actual angle traveled; stationary emotes,
partial laps, oscillation, off-season/forged completion offers and client timers
cannot grant it. Existing body-specific `/dance` animation works while circling.
Manual movement/combat, damage/death, root/stun/freezing, mounting, travel/zone
change, disconnect and event expiry cancel incomplete progress. Idle input/Stop
preserve it. Elite summons remain separate actions/landmarks.

Completion grants non-stacking **Festival Spirit: +10% attack and +50% XP for
7200 seconds**. Saved server Unix expiry continues offline; redoing both laps
refreshes to now+7200, without adding duration or bonuses. No cooldown. Earned
buff survives death, rebirth and season expiry until its timestamp; incomplete
dance progress never resumes. Old saves default to no buff. Hires/ambient actors
cannot earn it. Attack multiplies the normal post-gear/set attack stat by1.1;
XP adds50% of base XP alongside other bonuses through the shared monster/party/
quest reward path, without multiplying or consuming Rested. Existing PvP tuning
constants are unchanged; the normal attack stat applies through existing combat.

Summer Solstice NPCs now have inset hair flowers, Valentine NPCs hair/head
hearts and Easter NPCs decorated egg hairpieces (no rabbit-ear substitution).
They follow measured animated hair/head sockets and retain original identities,
face/name/service readability and four-city scope. These are native Canvas
accessories over the existing PixelFlow NPC sprites; no raster atlases or source
IDs were regenerated. Existing elite/mount art and all11mounts are preserved.

in seasonal_cities/sync-world. Remove old festival NPCs before regenerating city
dressing, otherwise their own centres fail the earlier clear-space validation.

clearing audit and reapplied afterwards. Valentine's guaranteed ON-CLAIM mount
remains distinct from other seasons' rare kill rolls. Existing title catalogs
and prefix/suffix rendering are preserved.
