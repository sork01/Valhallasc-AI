Pictures were uploaded to FileDrop as `valhallasc-shape-*`: `overview-<rule>` (all 23 zones, old square vs new outline), `z<NN>-<zone>-median` (one per zone), and `-nofloor` for Skaldholm, Nacrehold and the Yearwheel.

## Headline: what the scale rule costs

- **Two cities cannot fit under strict proportion.** Skaldholm (city 126x126) gets a 5,000-tile outline, Nacrehold (176x176) 12,100; no position inside the outline holds the city at true size. They need k >= 1.33 and 1.23 against the median 0.561.
- **The Yearwheel collapses to a tenth** of its area (400x400 -> about 123x177) with its 8 portals, season descents and the Black Hour door.
- **Early zones grow:** Greenmeadow 96 -> 236x135 (2.3x area), Crags 96 -> 203x195 (3.0x). Wyrdwood (0.49x), Cairnspine (0.37x), Tanglecrown (0.50x), Lapis Erg (0.51x) and Ran's Deep (0.51x) shrink to about half.
- **Spawn displacement under the stretch mapping:** 169 of 972 outdoor spawns land outside their outline (the Yearwheel 38 of 156, Wyrdwood 32 of 70). An upper bound: the generators re-lay content.
- **Portals:** 94 portals go to an atlas neighbour; 82 are within 25 tiles of their shared border after mapping (compass layouts already agree), 12 are not (Wyrdwood `sky_gate` 95, Bifrost `path_7_1` 48, Frostwake `frostwake_cairn_gate` 41, Crags `path_1_9` 41, Tanglecrown `tangle_worldheart_gate` 39, Bifrost `path_7_9` 38, ...).
- **Outline coarseness:** each outline has 10-22 vertices, so straight edges run 30-50 tiles at these sizes. The pictures show the raw polygons.
- Edge classes (shared / coast / outer land) are per zone in the second table; the painted PNG is 1758 px wide while the atlas frame is 2048, so sampling uses a 0.858 factor.
