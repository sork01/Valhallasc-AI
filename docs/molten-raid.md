# Molten raid design record

The detailed raid design (`RAID-RULES`) remains a separate unchecked task.
This page records only settled decisions needed for RAID-APPROACH, so they
do not have to be asked again. The owner's decisions are in TODO.md.

The raid door stands beyond Emberwake Caldera, a new public level-95–99
volcanic zone connected to Asterion (17). Emberwake is file zone 45; a future
raid must take the next free file zone at implementation time. The approach
is described and validated in [emberwake.md](emberwake.md).

The raid requires exactly ten level-99 fighters, any mix of humans and hired
mercenaries, fixed health, three private copies, no loot lockout. Nine hired
fighters are allowed inside the raid only. The outdoor approach retains the
current mercenary cap. RAID-PARTY must connect ten-player group support and
the approach Meeting Stone before its nine-hire service is ready.

Eldmarr, the Ember-Lord, kept the first forge beneath the world. Its heat kept
the world tree alive through the long winter. The gods built their hall on
his fire and promised him a seat at the table, then chained him beneath
Asterion's mountain instead. Nine Wardens, formerly his smiths and captains,
hold the chains. The heat has driven them mad, and the chains are failing.
Eldmarr intends to claim the promised hall by melting its roots.

Asterion's warm flagstones, cracked well and Runa Coalhand foreshadow the
forge. Emberwake's final quest lets the player hear Eldmarr's threat through
the sealed door. The raid entrance stays sealed through RAID-OPEN. Approach
music is 110 BPM dub-techno / half-time, distinct from the raid's planned
industrial techno. The remaining boss designs, balance baselines and loot
tables belong to RAID-RULES and their own implementation tasks.
